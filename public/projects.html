Polidesk

In development · v0.2.0

A little colleague for your screen. Polidesk sits at a desk in the bottom-right corner and keeps working while you do.

A Windows desktop app built with Electron.

Highlights

  • Transparent, frameless window pinned above all others
  • Drag the character anywhere, send it home from the tray menu
  • Stays visible across workspaces and full-screen apps
  • Portable .exe

Coming next

  • Head skins to choose from
  • Items you can throw at the character
  • A range of desks to work at
  • Characters that react to you more directly

Built with

  • Electron 31
  • Node.js
  • IPC & contextBridge
  • electron-builder
  • HTML / CSS / JS
